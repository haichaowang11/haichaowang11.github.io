Social anthropologist · University College London

I study how ethical and safety concepts are interpreted in practice, across China and the West.

My research follows how people in digital China form, justify and contest moral judgements. I now bring these methods to AI safety: how “safety” is understood across cultures, and how AI systems reason about plural values.

安全
ānquán
One word for both safety and security. What each side means by it shapes whether AI governance can be shared.

Research

In progress

AI safety across China and the West

How Chinese companies, standards bodies and public commentators use 安全 in practice, beyond official definitions. The question matters for international coordination: agreements depend on whether both sides mean the same thing by “safety”.

Pilot

Cultural reasoning in AI evaluation

A “thick reasoning” case bank built from my ethnographic material. Each case pairs a real ethical dilemma with a reference reading of how people actually reasoned about it, to test how Chinese and Western models handle culturally situated moral judgement.

Ongoing

Ethical life in digital China

Five years of fieldwork with grassroots women entrepreneurs in WeChat social commerce and with urban Hui Muslim communities. From this work I developed the concept of multisource ethical reasoning: how people draw on several, sometimes competing, sources of moral authority at once.

Methods & tools

Core methods

  • Long-term ethnography. Participant observation and in-depth interviews, including five years of fieldwork in China.
  • Chinese-language source analysis. Close reading of policy, platform and media texts in Chinese and English.
  • Rubric design for evaluating reasoning. Case-based rubrics that compare AI responses with how people actually reason in context.
  • Discourse and socio-linguistic analysis. How moral and technical concepts are framed, justified and contested in talk and text.
  • Quantitative analysis in R. Probability, statistics and regression.

Currently building

  • Python for handling and coding AI model transcripts.
  • Model APIs for running evaluation prompts across Chinese and Western models.
